Create and edit images

The picture tools listen for certain words. Use the words below and you get the right result first time. Press Use to put a prompt in your message box (change the details, then send), or copy it. marks prompts that need your picture attached.

Create a new picture

Start with a doing word and a picture word in the same sentence:

  • Doing words: create, make, generate, draw, design, give me — or in Hindi banao, bana do, bana ke do.
  • Picture words: image, photo, picture, logo, poster, wallpaper, banner, thumbnail, card, invitation, sticker, icon, avatar, illustration, Instagram post, story.
  • Or just describe the scene with two or more art words, like cinematic, 4k, watercolour, soft lighting.
  • A question such as “how to make a poster?” gets a written answer, not a picture.

Create an image of a golden retriever puppy playing in autumn leaves, warm sunlight, photorealistic.

Draw a cute cartoon robot reading a book, flat colours, plain white background.

Ek ladki ki image banao jo park mein bench par baithi hai, sunset, realistic.

Logos and words spelled exactly right

The picture model is great at art but can’t spell. When you say the words like this, the art is drawn without letters and your exact words are typed on top, so nothing is misspelled:

  • Logos: “logo for Name”, “logo with brand name Name”, or “logo called Name”. For a tagline add tagline "Your words".
  • Any picture with words: put the words in quotes — “a card that says "Happy Birthday Riya"”.
  • Write the name exactly how it should look — capital letters stay capital.

Create a logo for ClaudeChats, a friendly AI chat app, blue and purple gradient, modern.

Make a logo with brand name Sweet Crumbs and tagline "Fresh Cakes Daily", a cupcake symbol, pastel pink.

Create a birthday card image that says "Happy Birthday Riya" with balloons and confetti.

Posters, flyers and social posts

Say poster, flyer, banner or Instagram post, and include the real details — brand name, offer or price, phone, email, website. They are all printed exactly, in a clean layout.

Instagram poster for my bakery Sweet Crumbs – Fresh Cakes Daily, cakes from ₹299. Call 9876543210, visit sweetcrumbs.in. Warm pastel background.

Flyer for Yoga with Anu – morning batches at 6 AM, ₹999/month, call 9123456780, email anu@yogawithanu.in, calm green background.

Edit your own picture

  1. Attach the picture with the paperclip (or the camera).
  2. Start with an editing word: change, replace, remove, add, put, make it…, convert, crop, blur, brighten, enhance, rotate — or badlo, hatao, lagao, daalo, dikhao.
  3. Say one change at a time. Name the exact words if text should change.

Without an editing word, an attached picture is described, not changed. Edits draw the picture again, so small details can differ.

Change the background to a beach at sunset and keep the person exactly the same.

Replace the text "ChatGPT" with "Claude" and keep everything else the same.

Convert this photo into a Ghibli-style anime picture.

Is ladki ko cafe me dikhao, coffee cup ke saath.

Remove the background

Attach the picture (or ask right after one was made) and say remove the background, make the background transparent or bg hatao.

  • Plain backgrounds — one colour, like a logo on black or a product on white — are cut out exactly: every other pixel stays the same and the picture becomes truly transparent.
  • Busy photo backgrounds are drawn again with the change, so small details can differ.

Size and shape

  • Square (1:1): logo, icon, avatar, profile picture, “square”.
  • Instagram / Facebook post (4:5): “Instagram post”, “Facebook post”.
  • Tall (9:16): story, reel, status, phone wallpaper, “portrait”, “vertical”.
  • Poster (3:4): poster, flyer, invitation, A4, certificate.
  • Wide (16:9): YouTube thumbnail, banner, desktop wallpaper, “landscape”.
  • Or give it exactly: 1080×1350, 16:9, “size 4:5”.

YouTube thumbnail for a cooking video about 10-minute paneer recipes, bold and colourful, 16:9.

Phone wallpaper of a calm mountain lake at sunrise, soft pastel colours.

Tips for the best pictures

  • Describe it fully: subject, what it’s doing, place, style (photo, cartoon, 3D, watercolour), colours and mood.
  • Got a written answer instead of a picture? Start again with “Create an image of…”.
  • Brand names, prices and phone numbers come out right on logos and posters, and in quotes on anything else — see “Logos & exact words”.
  • New idea? Press New chat, so the earlier picture isn’t changed by mistake.
  • More images in the sidebar has ready-made styles: tap one, add your subject, send.
  • You can make up to 20 pictures a day.

Code-X: an AI coder in your terminal

Code-X works inside OpenCode, a free coding app that runs in your computer’s terminal. It reads your project, writes and fixes code, runs commands and explains what it did. Code-X is part of the premium plan.

  1. Open your profile menu (top right) and choose Code-X.
  2. Open a terminal — on Windows PowerShell or Command Prompt, on Mac or Linux Terminal — and paste the one command for your computer (below, or copy it from the Code-X panel). You don’t need to install anything first.
  3. The command shows a short code and opens a page in your browser. Check the code is the same as in your terminal and press Approve (log in if asked).
  4. Open a terminal in your project folder and type opencode. The first time, type /init so it studies your project.
  5. Describe what you want in plain words. Press Tab to switch between Plan (only reads and suggests) and Build (changes files). It asks before it changes anything.

powershell -NoProfile -Command "irm {{ site_origin }}/start-coding/setup.ps1 | iex"

curl -fsSL {{ site_origin }}/start-coding/setup.sh | bash

Deep research

  1. Press Deep research in the message box (it lights up), then type your question.
  2. A research plan appears. Edit the steps if you like, then press Start — or it starts by itself after a minute.
  3. It searches the web, reads the pages and writes a report with numbered sources. Download it as PDF, Word or Markdown.

Compare electric scooters available in India under ₹1.5 lakh: range, price, charging time and service network.

Web search

  1. Press the globe button — the message box turns green.
  2. Ask about anything recent: news, prices, results, dates. The answer uses live web results.
  3. Press the globe again to turn it off.

What are the latest updates on the Indian stock market today?

Attach files and photos

  1. Press the paperclip to attach images, PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, code files or audio (up to 50 MB each), a video (up to 100 MB), or the camera to take a photo.
  2. For a document, choose Show details to ask about it, or Start coding to have it changed.
  3. Then say what you want: summarise it, explain it, find mistakes, translate it.

Summarise this file in 5 bullet points and list any dates or deadlines in it.

Read the text in this photo and solve the question step by step.

Convert a file

  1. Press the ⇄ convert button at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Choose a file: PDF, Word, text, Markdown, CSV, Excel or a picture (PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP).
  3. Pick the format you want — for example PDF to Word, Excel to CSV, or JPG to PNG — and the new file downloads.
  • Converting runs on our own server. The file is not sent to the AI and is not kept.

Reply to one answer

  1. Press Reply under any answer — it is quoted above the message box.
  2. To quote only part of it, select that text first, then press Reply.
  3. Type your follow-up (“why?”, “shorter”, “give an example”) and send. Press × or Esc to cancel.

Answers in your language

  1. Press Reply in at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Search or pick one of 32 languages — Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati and many more.
  3. Every answer now comes in that language, and the microphone, Listen and voice calls use it too. Pick English to switch back.

Voice and calls

  1. Press the microphone and speak — your words are typed into the box.
  2. Press the phone to start a voice call and talk with the AI; you can interrupt it while it speaks.
  3. Press Listen under any answer to hear it read aloud.
  4. After a call, open My calls in the profile menu to read the transcript, play the recording, download it as text or PDF, or delete it.

Developer API: use the AI from your own app

  1. Open your profile menu and choose Get API.
  2. Press Request API access. You get the same models you can already use in the chat.
  3. Press Generate API key and copy it straight away — it is shown only once. Keep it secret like a password.
  4. Send a POST request with the key, as below. Replace quick with one of the model names listed in Get API.

curl -X POST {{ site_origin }}/api/v1/chat/ -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"model\": \"quick\", \"message\": \"Hello!\"}"

  • The answer comes back as JSON with a reply field.
  • You can also send a picture to ask about it, choose the reply language, and make or change pictures. The How to use tab in Get API has copy-ready examples in curl, PowerShell, Python and JavaScript.
  • The Overview tab shows your requests today, the last 7 days, each model’s share and your recent requests. The Models tab lists each model’s key: change "model" to switch.
  • On the free plan, API messages come out of your free messages. Each key also has a per-minute and a daily limit.
  • Lost the key, or think someone else has it? Press Regenerate API key — the old one stops working at once.

Tips for better results

  • Be specific. Say who it is for, how long, and the format: “a 200-word email to my manager asking for leave on Friday”.
  • Give the facts you have. Names, prices, dates and figures you type are used as they are; nothing is made up for them.
  • Build on the answer. Ask “shorter”, “more formal”, “as a table” or “add examples” instead of starting over.
  • Start a New chat for a new topic, so earlier messages don’t mix in.
  • Something wrong? Press Retry, Edit your message, or Report it under the answer.
